FRIDA Survey

FRIDA (Finding Rotation In ultra-Diffuse gAlaxies) is a unique observing program providing high-resolution H I kinematics using MeerKAT and the VLA, together with ultra-deep optical imaging using GTC, for a sample of gas-rich ultra-diffuse galaxies.

This page will be updated regularly to include data products derived from the program.
