FRIDA

FRIDA (Finding Rotation In ultra-Diffuse gAlaxies) is a unique observing program providing high-resolution H I kinematics (using MeerKAT and the VLA) and ultra-deep optical imaging (using GTC) for a sample of gas-rich ultra-diffuse galaxies.

This website will be updated regularly to include data products derived from the program.
