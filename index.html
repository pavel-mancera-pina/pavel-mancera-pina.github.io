About Me

Hi, I am Pavel. Welcome to my website! I am an astrophysicist interested in galaxy formation and evolution. I am a NWO-Veni Fellow at Leiden Observatory studying the dynamics of galaxies through cosmic time.

Research Interests

I have very broad interests in galaxy formation and evolution. My expertise is in the kinematics and dynamics of galaxies (especially discs), but in general I am very interested in the processes regulating the baryon cycle of galaxies and the connection between the observed properties of galaxies and the theoretical expectations for their host dark matter haloes.
